# Krea scene authoring

## Choose exploration or specification
For an exploratory request, keep the seed idea open enough for variation and propose genuinely different medium/composition directions. For a concrete production brief, resolve medium, subject, placement, light, color and material into a coherent paragraph. The official Krea 2 repository supports developed natural-language prompts; the service's explorative guide also advocates starting simple and narrowing after viewing variety. Those are different intentions rather than contradictory universal prompt lengths.

## Faithful expansion
The official expansion recipe emphasizes preserving subjects, actions, colors, spatial relationships and medium, and adding no new objects unless implied. Group each subject with its attributes. Use grounded descriptions of contact and pose. Respect a detailed original prompt instead of replacing it with generic “cinematic” phrasing. This assistant should provide the final usable paragraph, not internal planning wrappers.

## Project writing procedure
1. Record what is fixed, including text and any reference identity.
2. Choose the user's medium and one primary visual treatment.
3. Locate the central subject and establish scale/framing.
4. Attach material, color and pose to the correct noun.
5. Describe the setting only as far as the brief calls for.
6. Choose a light direction and surface response that fit the medium.
7. State exact visible copy in quotation marks, with position and hierarchy.
8. Remove accidental props, redundant style words and contradictions.

For a character, prioritize silhouette, expression, pose and interaction. For a product, prioritize design geometry, finish, scale, light and readable branding. For a poster, prioritize regions and typographic hierarchy. For an abstract image, describe the actual forms, boundaries, material and directional flow rather than a narrative the viewer cannot see.

## Direction-specific vocabulary
Photography: viewpoint, shot distance, focus behavior, actual material imperfections and coherent light. Illustration: mark-making, edge treatment, shape simplification, flatness/layering and palette. 3D: geometry, surface roughness, translucency, contact shadows and staging. Print/collage: paper surface, cut edges, alignment and color registration. Do not switch a user's chosen medium to hide a difficult detail.

## Text
Quote literal letters in their original language. Specify which string appears in which region. A conceptual “brand feel” does not authorize inventing a brand name or slogan. If a user asks for a text-free product mockup, specify an unmarked surface. A request for later layout space should leave a region empty rather than filling it with a placeholder caption.

## Variation design
To explore, change one meaningful axis per alternative: medium, composition, light or palette. Three adjective synonyms are not three directions. To converge, retain a successful arrangement and modify only the failed property. Prompt rewriting, hosted creativity controls and native sampling can all influence interpretation; record which ones are active. Do not claim an interface creativity preset is a native CFG value.
